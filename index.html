Stagelight

Turn any song from YouTube Music into a full-screen lyric show with an epic title card and visuals that pulse on the beat.

Example song

In YouTube Music, tap Share, then Copy link, on a song or playlist and paste it here. Stagelight plays it, works out each song and syncs the lyrics by itself.

or set up a song yourself

Copy the lyrics from the Lyrics tab in YouTube Music and paste them here. Timestamped lines like [01:12.40] play on their own; plain lines move on when you tap the screen.

Scene

Tap the button in time with the song a few times and the visuals lock onto its tempo.

Or let the app hear the music, and the visuals hit on every beat by themselves.

Play the song in YouTube Music, then switch back here. On a phone, music keeps playing in the background with YouTube Music Premium; otherwise play it from another device or a speaker. The microphone works best when the music comes from a speaker.
